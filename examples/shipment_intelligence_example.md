# Shipment Intelligence — Example Output

This is a representative example of the AI analysis generated by the project.

## Executive Summary

The shipment dataset contains 30 shipments with an average delivery time of 3.7 days and total shipping costs of 24,900.

The distribution shows 16 delivered shipments, 7 in-transit shipments, and 7 pending shipments.

## Key Findings

- Hyderabad has the highest shipment volume with 8 shipments.
- Mumbai has the highest average delivery time at 4.5 days.
- Mumbai also has the highest average shipping cost at 987.5.
- Heavy shipments represent 28 of the 30 shipments.
- Delivered shipments account for 16 of the 30 shipments.

## Operational Attention Areas

Mumbai has both the highest average delivery time and the highest average shipping cost among the cities in the dataset.

The large proportion of heavy shipments may also require attention because shipment weight can affect transportation cost and operational handling.

## Recommended Actions

- Investigate the causes of longer delivery times in Mumbai.
- Review shipping-cost drivers for Mumbai shipments.
- Monitor pending and in-transit shipments.
- Review the operational impact of the high proportion of heavy shipments.
